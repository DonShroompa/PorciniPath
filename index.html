    About this map
    
    
    A non-commercial hobby project: an estimate of where seven edible mushrooms may grow in the Netherlands, built
      from open data. It says where, never when, and it is not a field guide.
    

    
    Disclaimer
    
    
      
      
    • Not validated. All scores are educated guesses from the ecology of each species. A first check against
        precise observations supported some species and not others (for porcini it found no skill). Use the map as a
        search aid, nothing more.
      • 
      
    • Identification is your responsibility. Never eat a mushroom you have not identified with certainty,
        with a recent field guide or an expert; apps are not reliable. Some edible species have poisonous lookalikes.
        Do not eat chicken of the woods growing on yew. The author accepts no liability for any consequence of using
        this map.
      • 
      
    • Picking rules are information, not legal advice. There is no general right to forage in the
        Netherlands: mushrooms belong to the landowner, and owners and municipal bylaws set the rules. The rules shown
        are incomplete, may be out of date, and boundaries are approximate. Signs on site always take precedence.
      • 
      
    • Private ground. Many trees shown stand in gardens, on estates or on other private land. Being on this
        map gives no right of access: ask the owner.
      • 
      
    • Tree data comes from public registers and may be outdated (trees felled, notes without a date). A
        fungus recorded by tree inspectors is a past note, not a current find.
      • 
      
    • Finds are other people's observations; they show where people looked, not where a species is absent.
      • 
      
    • Please pick little, for your own use, and leave the place as you found it.
      •
